Aangan — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026
Publisher: Kunba
Contact: Ateeshay.jain@gmail.com

The short version. Aangan is local-first. Household data is stored on your iPhone and, when iCloud is available, privately synced through your own Apple account by default. You can pause sync in Settings. Kunba operates no household-data backend and does not collect, sell or use your data for ads, analytics or tracking.

1. Who we are

Aangan is published by Kunba. Version 1.4 has no Kunba account, server login, paid verification service, cloud-AI service, advertising or analytics.

2. Information you can store

Aangan lets you store homes and members; staff names, phone numbers, attendance, salary and advances; bills, expenses, payment methods and ledgers; UPI or bank details you choose to record; assets, warranties, vehicle papers and documents; meals, contacts, to-dos, Lists, calendar events, laundry and handoffs; and photos, scans or PDFs. Health information is optional too: a family member's blood group (for the Emergency screen) and the meals read from a diet chart you import.

Government IDs and financial details are optional. Aangan does not require an Aadhaar or PAN number to use the app.

3. Where information lives

On your iPhone. Records are stored in Aangan's private app databases and files. Local Lists stay only on this iPhone in version 1.4. iOS data protection encrypts app storage at rest; attachment writes use complete file protection. From version 1.5, the salary slips, receipts and export files Aangan prepares for you to share are protected the same way and removed afterwards.

In iPhone search and Siri. From version 1.5, Aangan gives the iPhone's own search (Spotlight) and Siri the names of the staff, bills and vendors in the home you have open, so that you can find and open them from outside the app and use Siri phrases such as "what do I owe". Only the name and what kind of thing it is are given: never an amount, a phone number, a UPI ID or a document. This stays on your iPhone and is handled by Apple's search and Siri under Apple's policies; nothing is sent to Kunba. There is no switch for it in this version. Search results may appear on a locked iPhone if your iPhone's own settings allow search there.

Siri is also given the names of the dishes on this week's meal plan, so that you can ask for a recipe by name. Two Siri answers work while your iPhone is locked, so that you can ask from the kitchen: a recipe from Aangan's own recipe book, and "what's for lunch", which says the dish planned for a meal exactly as it was typed in the meal plan. Neither says a person's name, an amount or a meal's note. Anyone near your iPhone can ask for these. Everything else Siri does with Aangan, such as marking attendance, recording clothes given to the dhobi or saying what is due, asks you to unlock your iPhone first.

With "Ask Aangan", Siri hears your request as it does any Siri command. Aangan does not store, log or send what you said; only what you confirm is saved, as a record in the app.

In your Calendar and in reminders, if you turn them on. Export to Apple Calendar (off by default) writes the names of services, bills, items under warranty and documents with an expiry date into a calendar called "Aangan". Other apps you have given calendar access can read it, and it syncs with your calendar account. Show details in reminders (off by default) puts names and amounts into reminder banners, including on the lock screen. From version 1.5, a document you have locked is not named in either place.

In your iCloud. When iCloud is available, household sync runs by default through Apple CloudKit. Pause or resume it under More → Settings → iCloud sync. Data is held under your Apple account and processed by Apple under its iCloud terms, not sent to a Kunba backend.

If you deliberately share a home, Apple makes that home's data available only to participants you invite. Every participant is a co-owner of that home: they can read and change everything in it, including documents, photos and scans, and a copy is stored on their iPhone. There are no read-only roles in this version. Do not invite someone you do not trust.

Removing a participant stops new changes reaching them. The copy already on their iPhone stays there until they leave or delete the home in Aangan.

4. What Kunba does not collect

  • We do not receive your household records, photos, documents or searches.
  • We do not use analytics, telemetry, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent or broker personal information.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.

Apple may process iCloud, App Store and opt-in Apple diagnostics data under Apple's own policies. Kunba does not receive private household content through those services.

Speak to Aangan. The mic button on Today listens only after you tap it, and stops when you finish speaking, when you close its screen or when you leave the app. What you say is turned into text on your iPhone: Aangan requires on-device speech recognition and does not listen at all on an iPhone that cannot do it on the device, so the audio is not sent to Apple, to Kunba or to anyone else. Aangan never keeps the audio. The words are kept only where you save them: the items you add to a list, and the note of a spend, which starts as the sentence you said and which you can change before saving. Otherwise, once Aangan has acted on the words or you close the screen, they are gone. To help the iPhone hear your own words, Aangan gives its on-device recogniser the names of your staff, your deliveries and the dishes on your meal plan each time you tap the mic. Nothing is saved until you have seen what Aangan understood and tapped Save, and no payment is recorded by voice. You can type instead of speaking, and you can turn either permission off in iPhone Settings at any time. When iPhone first asks for Speech Recognition, the text Apple adds to that question is Apple's own.

Photo imports use Apple's system PhotosPicker, which gives Aangan only the items you select without broad Photo Library authorization. Release does not request contacts or location permission.

6. Security

  • Photos, scans and PDFs you attach use iOS complete file protection. From version 1.5 so do the slips and export files Aangan prepares for sharing.
  • Records you lock require Face ID or the device passcode before they are shown on this iPhone. The lock is a display gate, not extra encryption: a locked record still syncs to your iCloud and to the co-owners of a shared home, who can unlock it on their own iPhones. The lock covers the document itself: its name, issuer, policy number and expiry date can still be seen in lists inside the app and in an export.
  • The app-switcher snapshot is covered while Aangan is inactive.
  • iCloud transport and storage security are provided by Apple.
  • Aangan has no third-party package dependencies or embedded server secrets.

7. Hand-offs you initiate

Sharing a slip or export, opening a UPI app, WhatsApp, Mail, the dialler, a brand website (a brand's registration page opens in a browser window inside Aangan) or a YouTube recipe search (the dish name is the search term), copying an item's warranty details to paste into a brand's form, and sending a warranty-claim message happen only after you tap. The receiving service then applies its own privacy policy.

8. Access, export and deletion

  • You can access, correct and export household records directly in Aangan.
  • Deleting an owned home removes its records and the attachments they point to locally and sends deletions through that home's iCloud sync. Text that Aangan recognised when you scanned a receipt is kept in a log on this iPhone and is removed only by Erase all data on this device.
  • Leaving another person's shared home removes your participation without deleting the owner's records.
  • Erase all data on this device removes local databases, local Lists, attachments, settings, reminders, managed calendar events and the names given to iPhone search. From version 1.5 it also removes slips and export files Aangan had prepared for sharing. It does not delete other family phones or your existing iCloud copy; signing back in may download synced data again.
  • Deleting the app removes its local container but may not delete data already in iCloud or copies exported elsewhere.

Kunba cannot access or delete content in your private iCloud account for you.

9. Children

Aangan is intended for adults managing a household. It is not directed at children, and Kunba does not knowingly collect children's data.

10. Changes and contact

Material policy changes will update the effective date and, where appropriate, be disclosed in the app or release notes.
